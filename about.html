About Salone Bites

We are passionate about Sierra Leonean food. Our mission is to bring the flavours, stories and traditions of our local cuisine to your table. We support local farmers and cooks, and we celebrate the rich culinary heritage of Salone.

Our mission

To promote Sierra Leonean cuisine, support local communities, and give everyone the chance to experience the authentic taste of Salone.

Why Salone Bites?

Because we believe food is more than just a meal. It is a connection to our culture, our people and our heritage. We share authentic local dishes, trusted restaurants and the rich food traditions of Sierra Leone.
